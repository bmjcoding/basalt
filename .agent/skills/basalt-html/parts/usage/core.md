# core: layout, type, panels, buttons

**Layout:** `.page` (`.narrow`), `.stack` and `.cluster` (`style="--gap: var(--basalt-space-3)"`), `.grid` (`--min: 220px`), `.split` (`.aside`: a 320px sidebar), `.section` > `.section-head` (a section brings 48px of space above itself, 32 under 720: put sections straight in `.page`, not in a gapped `.stack`; a line under its title is `p.section-description`), `.spacer`, `.hide-narrow`, `.divider`, `.divide-y`, `.footer`, `.tap`, `.skip-link`, `.visually-hidden`.
**Type:** `h1`–`h4`, `.display`, `.text-5xl` (56/60; give it room at 390), `.lead`, `.eyebrow`, `.muted`, `.faint`, `.small`, `.mono`, `.num`, `.prose`, inline `code`. `h2` is 24/30. `.lead` (16/24, under a report's `h1`) and `.prose` are 34rem on the row's start edge; `.page` is the 72rem row. A site page's display type, lead (`.text-lead`, 20/28), row and sections are `type-layout`.
**Panels:** `.panel`, `.card`, `.panel-pad`, `.panel-head`, `.float` (+ `.modal`). `.float` is only for what leaves the flow; never to promote a panel. `.card.lg` and `.panel-pad.lg` take wider padding from 720px up.
**Buttons:** `.btn[data-variant=primary|ghost|danger]` (default secondary), `data-size=sm|lg`, `data-icon-only`, `aria-busy`.
- **At an edge:** a ghost `.btn` that starts or ends a head, bar or row takes `data-edge="start"` or `"end"`: its glyph or words sit on the head's line and its box hangs into the padding, only as far as the head's `--basalt-edge-room` (its padding less any safe area; a head of your own sets it, `style="--basalt-edge-room: var(--basalt-space-4)"`). A `.panel-head`'s last ghost `.btn`, and a `.page > .section > .section-head`'s, hang by themselves. Never a margin by hand.

- **Busy** is `aria-busy="true"` on the button (label in a `<span>`): the label hides and a ring sits over it, so the width holds.
- **Press:** a press is a colour step, and `.btn` also scales to .98 (100ms, ease-out) while pressed, never when disabled or busy and never under reduced motion; nothing else scales on press.
- **Links on touch:** a standalone link (a nav link, a footer link, a link on its own line) gets a 44px hit box on touch with no change to layout; a link inside running text (`p`, `li`, `.prose`, `.small`, `.hint`) stays inline. A link already 44px tall in its part (the bar, a nav row) needs nothing. Add `.tap` to a link the CSS can't tell is standalone.
- **Skip link:** `<a class="skip-link" href="#main">Skip to content</a>`, first in `<body>`: unseen until focus, then floating, 8px under a top bar's real edge.
- **Print:** the script prints in the light theme and goes back after; the skip link, theme toggle and copy buttons are not printed (the top bar is: static and opaque).
- **A dot between words:** `In design<span class="dot-sep" data-spaced><span class="visually-hidden">, </span></span>Pilot planned` reads "In design, Pilot planned" and draws the dot; `data-spaced` draws a space each side. Never an `aria-hidden` dot beside a hidden comma.
- **A brand SVG file** (from `brand/`): `data-brand` on its `<svg>`; compose hides it, drops its `<title>` and the CSS gives it no pointer. The link round it has `aria-label`.
- **A value and its unit** in separate elements: a space between them in the markup, or `<span class="visually-hidden"> </span>`.
- **Words:** “ ” `’`, not in code or URLs; one `…`, only on an item that asks for more ("Rename…"). `&nbsp;` joins value and unit, clock and period or zone, month and day, quarter and year, `⌘&nbsp;K`, a two-word name. `translate="no"` on inline `code`, names, identifiers. Locale `en-US` unless the reader's language says otherwise.
- **Figures:** `time`, `.badge`, `.status` are tabular; `.num` for amounts.
- **Date and age:** `<time datetime="2026-11-01">Nov&nbsp;1</time><span class="dot-sep" data-spaced><span class="visually-hidden">, </span></span><span class="date-age-age">11&nbsp;days ago</span>`, never the age alone.
- **Script:** `Basalt.announce(text)` speaks to screen readers. `Basalt.safeHref(h)` returns `h` when it is relative, http, https, mailto or tel, else `null`: leave a refused link out. The modules escape the words a page passes them (entries, groups, items, chart JSON) and draw a refused `href` as a button.
- **Icons:** `svg.icon` is what the script makes of an `<i data-icon>`; never write it yourself.
