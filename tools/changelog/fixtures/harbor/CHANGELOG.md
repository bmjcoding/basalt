## 2.0.0 - 2026-12-07

**Responders sign in with their own account**

Each responder now signs in with their own account, through your company's identity provider.

### For admins

In Settings, open Sign-in and connect your identity provider. Shared room codes stop working on Jan 15, 2027.

Single sign-on replaces shared room codes, with a sign-in page for your company's identity provider.

## 1.4.0 - 2026-11-23

**A monitor can open an incident room**

A monitor that can send a webhook can now open an incident room when its alarm fires. The room shows the alarm, the service it names and the checks already run.

### For responders

On Home, open an incident marked "Opened by a monitor". The first message in the room is the alarm, with a link to the monitor.

### For admins

In Settings, open Integrations and add a webhook. Paste its address into your monitor.

Monitors can open incident rooms through a webhook, and Home marks the incidents they open. The open incident list keeps its place, and two fixes cover the lobby and search.

### Added

- Home: Added an "Opened by a monitor" mark to incidents that a webhook declared ([#131](https://github.com/example/harbor/pull/131))
- Integrations: Added webhook declarations so a monitor can open an incident room when its alarm fires ([#131](https://github.com/example/harbor/pull/131), Linear [OPS-204](https://linear.app/example/issue/OPS-204))

### Changed

- Home: Improved the open incident list so your scroll position stays fixed when an incident arrives above it ([#132](https://github.com/example/harbor/pull/132))

### Fixed

- Lobby: Fixed "Waiting to be admitted" staying on screen after the commander had admitted you, until you reloaded the page ([#133](https://github.com/example/harbor/pull/133))
- Past incidents: Fixed search missing an update the commander had published in the last minute ([#134](https://github.com/example/harbor/pull/134))
